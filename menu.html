Food Allergen Information

Our mission is to ensure you can trust the food you choose to eat. Sigree's kitchen is not a dedicated allergen-free facility.

With limited kitchen space, shared preparation benches, ovens, grills, fryers and other appliances, the possibility of human error during busy service, and ingredients from suppliers that carry "may contain" warnings, we are unable to guarantee that any dish is completely free of allergens.

If you have a severe allergy, please talk to our team before ordering.
